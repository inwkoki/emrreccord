Sign in with your ED account to keep your patient notes.

Accounts use a username + PIN (no email), so there's no automatic reset. If you can still sign in on any device, change your PIN anytime in ⚙️ Account. If you're fully locked out, ask the system owner to reset it in the Firebase console (Authentication → find your user → Reset password).

⚠️ Prototype only — not a certified/HIPAA·PDPA-compliant EMR. Do not enter real identifiable patient data until properly secured. Use bed numbers, not names. A PIN is convenience-grade security only.
